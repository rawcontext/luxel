VERSION 1.5.2

1. ACCESS
Luxel is a native Mac menu-bar recorder for macOS 26.0+. It has no Dock icon or initial window. Launch it from Applications and use its menu-bar icon. No account, credentials, subscription, demo data, or sample file is required.

2. VERSION 1.5.2 CHANGES
This maintenance update fixes screen recordings failing to save when screen content remains static and then changes. It improves reliable saving of display, window, and selected-area recordings.

3. REVIEW STEPS
1) Record a display, window, or selected area for at least 30 seconds. Leave the recorded content still for a few seconds, then move or scroll content inside the capture. Click Stop once.
2) Confirm a successful recording opens in the editor and plays normally. Start another recording to confirm the controls remain available.
3) Make and stop an audio-only recording, then confirm it opens and plays normally.

4. PRIVACY AND PERMISSIONS
This update adds no account, network service, data collection, or new permission. Recording, transcription, title generation, editing, and export remain local to the Mac.
