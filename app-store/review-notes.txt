VERSION 1.3.1

1. ACCESS
Luxel is a native Mac menu-bar recorder for macOS 26.0+. It has no Dock icon or initial window. Launch it from Applications and use its menu-bar icon. No account, credentials, subscription, demo data, or sample file is required.

2. VERSION 1.3.1 CHANGE
Performance improvements and bug fixes.

3. REVIEW STEPS
1) Start a screen or audio-only recording from the menu bar.
2) Stop the recording after a short interval.
3) Confirm the saved recording opens and plays normally.

4. PRIVACY AND PERMISSIONS
This update adds no account, network service, data collection, or new permission. Recording, transcription, editing, and export remain local to the Mac.
